Recording and comparison details

The student video is the original successful benchmark record from October 28. BF16 and the prior QAD baseline were each evaluated three times on November 3; the videos show their first runs. No teacher guidance was added. The original student run was not replaced by a new run.

Student time estimates the interval from first screenshot saved to outcome saved. Baseline timings measure an episode after warmup/reset, including model calls and browser actions. Dates, image loading, and timing boundaries differ, so this is a historical execution comparison, not a controlled acceleration measurement. The original welcome illustration was unavailable and is preserved as recorded.

BF16 and the student use brownie model lineage; the earlier three-update QAD baseline starts from Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct. This is not a matched-training ablation. Success is the native environment evaluator’s result. The student’s final screenshot was not separately captured; its last recorded view is retained.
